# Independent TTL boundary finding

Original finding at `app/native-existing-file-authorities.mjs` SHA-256 `4EBE1F150B5DE37E75F5F7F9763D26E313F1449BF82EF3413534D4FFAE66EF59`: `authorizePublication` called `expected()`, which called `eligible()`. `eligible()` revalidated the startup service-capacity observation and capability evidence at current time. `daemon/src/native-account-observation.ts` `readIssuedNativeServiceAuthentication` (line 117) rejects observations after `validUntilMs`; `daemon/src/capability-admission.ts` (line 99) denies evidence at age `>= maxAgeMs`. A valid launched implementation whose run crossed the remaining TTL could therefore have a succeeded native receipt and verified cleanup but receive publication denial. The driver would block with `change_publication_unresolved` (`app/orchestration-driver.mjs` around lines 527–554, 602). This was a safe denial of an in-flight completion, not an authority bypass.

The correct boundary is fresh admission for selection, claim, and launch, followed by durable launch/plan/account lineage, receipt, cleanup, target-byte, and run-deadline checks at publication. A focused fake-clock test should cross the startup observation TTL after implementation cleanup, prove publication still commits, then prove new verifier launch admission is denied while the evidence is stale. This document records the finding and test needed; it does not claim a fix or a passing run.
