# Decision-backed recovery claim limits discovery

Done means a new real-ledger/store fixture issues a valid recovery decision and measures its later replacement claim after (a) the bound deadline and (b) cumulative attempt capacity has been consumed. It must also establish whether a production-created recovery scope can exist without the retry contract that the store uses for final caps.

Attempt cap: two offline fixture corrections. Every pass runs, from `daemon/`, `npx vitest run test/integration-recovery-claim-limits.test.ts`. This discovery changes no product source and runs no shared build, native helper, model/provider, network, or live workflow.

If existing final admission rejects both cases, the hypothesis is retracted with exact errors and zero-write evidence. If either claim is admitted, record the before behavior and propose the smallest scoped fix before editing production.
