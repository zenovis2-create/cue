# Pending backend design: cold start and separately authorized exploration

Status: design/discovery only; not implemented or checked complete. Source: INTEGRATION_SPEC section3 selection principles, selection/policy.ts, policy-store.ts, orchestration/engine.ts, budget.ts and evaluation/enrollment.ts. Existing v1 rejects unknown estimates; evaluation enrollment binds a cohort before approval but has no candidate identity or authority to spend on exploration.

Preserve v1 canonical JSON/digests and decision validation. A versioned opt-in policy must bind an explicit conservative default and separately authorized exploration identity. A default cannot synthesize missing prices, quality guarantees or qualification; retain normal capability, quality, privacy, manual-pin and cost/time limits. Distinguish absent performance samples from absent safe cost/quality bounds. Unknown required bounds remain denied.

A separate append-only exploration authorization/enrollment/reservation store must bind run, policy revision/digest, candidate, currency/unit and approved upper bound. Use the existing real engine claim transaction for atomic rollback/idempotency. Exploration allocation must not silently draw from ordinary-only authorization; nevertheless total run accounting must still include every exploration cost. Treat exploration as an additional approved sublimit of the total authorized spend, not a way to remove an attempt from aggregate accounting. Do not blindly apply the initial research suggestion to replace ordinary accounting with an isolated charge ledger.

Before implementation, resolve the exact compatibility contract for dual total/exploration reservations and quality bounds; map all policy/decision validators, engine replay and cost/report readers. Proposed gates: v1 compatibility, qualified default selection, no unknown-bound bypass, missing/zero/exhausted/foreign budget or enrollment blocks before launch, concurrent reservations never exceed either authorized cap, same attempt replay no duplicate charge, later claim failure rolls both reservations back, old policies remain immutable after reopen. UI/host authorization and actual price ingestion remain separate production integration work.
